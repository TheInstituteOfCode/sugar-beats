PERSONAL DATA TERMS AND POLICY

INTODUCTION

SugarBEATS Beach Club recognizes the importance of theating out guests personal data with respect and confidentiality. This privacy policy and terms (“Policy”) explains how to collect, use, store, secure and share your personal data. This Policy also explains how to protect your personal data and how we handle incidents and data breaches, in accordance with Undang-undang Nomor 27 tahun 2022 tentang Perlindungan Data Pribadi, standard practices of the Indonesian hospitality industry.

PERSONAL DATA STATEMENT

Your personal data is very important to us. We want you to trust that any personal data you provide to us for our services (“Services”) is collected, used, stored and secured properly.

This privacy statement explains who we are and how we collect, use, store and secure your personal data.

HOW WE COLLECT PERSONAL DATA

Our purposes for collectiong tour personal data are, as follow:

  1. To process reservations and check-in;

  1. To verify identity;

  2. For payment purposes;

  3. To send cofirmations and promotions;

  4. To improve services and experiences;

  5. To comply with applicable regulations.

We collect you personal data in various ways, as follows:

  1. When you make a reservation;

  2. When you provide feedback, ask questions, make transactions, or use our services and facilities. This includes booking a room or dining and contacting our call center;

  3. When you register for alerts or subscribe to our mailing lists or notification services;

  4. When you visit our locations, such as restaurants and beach clubs;

  5. When you register for tickets, credit facilities, or memberships relevant to our available programs;

  6. When your image or voice is recorded by cameras or other devices installed at our locations;

  7. When you use our self-service kiosks, websites, mobile applications, or interact on our social media pages;

  8. When you participate in our surveys, interviews, contests, or events;

  9. From third-party sources who may lawfully disclose your personal data to us (such as background check providers, event organizers, partners, travel and ticketing agents, or booking platforms);

  10. If required by law to collect our personal data.

The personal data we collect is, as follows:

  1. Identity: Full name, ID number, date of birth, nationality, telephone number and email address

  2. Reservations: Booking details and special preferences

  3. Payment:Credit/debit card information or other payment method

  4. Usage:Mobile application data, IP address, device and browser information

HOW WE USE PERSONAL DATA

We use your personal data, as follows:

  1. To process and manage feedback, inquiries, transactions, or use of our services and facilities;

  2. To invite you to participate in surveys, interviews, contests, or attend our events;

  3. To manage and maintain your profile or membership account across our various businesses;

  4. To provide assistance and cooperation with relevant stakeholders or third parties to identify and prevent illegal activity and other misconduct. This includes identity verification, financial and background screening, money laundering prevention, anti-fraud and managing admission or access to our locations, such as restaurants and beach clubs;

  5. To comply with and implement applicable legal and regulatory obligations and recommendations;

  6. To facilitate the registration, administration and issuance of relevant tickets or rewards for our membership program;

  7. To assess and process your credit eligibility application and other financial matters, such as debt collection;

  8. To facilitate the management and supervision of our locations;

  9. To monitor your activity while using our services and facilities so that we can conduct research and data analytics to improve and personalize your experience with us. We may also monitor individuals who are in our facilities to ensure we provide appropriate facilities, allocate adequate resources and provide appropriate services;

  10. To facilitate surveillance, if necessary and other emergency response efforts.

  11. To conduct our marketing and communication activities to ensure they are relevant to your interests, including profiling you, targeted advertising and holding significant promotions and discounts

  12. To ensure that our facilities and services are used responsibly;

  13. To ensure safety and security, such as by using cameras and facial recognition technology for identification and access management;

  14. To detect, prevent, or address security or technical issues related to the services provided to you through our systems;

  15. For other reasonable purposes related to improving and providing our services and facilities

  1. HOW WE STORE AND SECURE PERSONAL DATA

We store and secure your personal data, as follows:

  1. Encrypting data;

  2. Establishing server security systems;

  3. Conducting regular server security audits;

  4. Restricting internal access;

  5. Protecting with antivirus software

  1. HOW WE SHARE PERSONAL DATA

We share your personal data, as follows:

  1. With our affiliates, subsidiaries and associated companies for relevant purposes;

  2. With our agents, partners, service providers, suppliers and business partners to assist us in providing our facilities and services;

  3. With our professional advisors;

  4. With government agencies or authorities, operators, or industry bodies that require, permit, advise, or expect us or our affiliates to disclose your personal data;

  5. When sharing or disclosure is necessary as required or permitted by law, including:

  1. Responding to emergencies that may threaten the life, health, or safety of people;

  2. Establishing, exercising, or defending our legal rights;

  3. Identifying or investigating illegal activities and errors, whether they have occurred or are still suspected.

  1. HOW WE HANDLE DATA INCIDENTS AND BREACHES

We will coordinate and consult with the necessary parties in the event of a personal data breach, ensuring our responsibility to handle incidents and breaches of personal data.

  1. TERMS AND POLICY CHANGES

This Policy applies in accordance with applicable notices, contractual clauses, and other agreements regarding our collection, use, storage, security, sharing and handling of your personal data. We may amend this Policy to reflect changes in our methods for collecting, using, storing, securing, sharing and handling your personal data without prior notice

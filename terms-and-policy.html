PERSONAL DATA STATEMENT

Your personal data is very important to us. We want you to trust that any personal data you provide to us for our services (“Services”) is collected, used, stored and secured properly.

This privacy statement explains who we are and how we collect, use, store and secure your personal data.

HOW WE COLLECT PERSONAL DATA

Our purposes for collectiong tour personal data are, as follow:

  1. To process reservations and check-in;
  2. To verify identity;
  3. For payment purposes;
  4. To send cofirmations and promotions;
  5. To improve services and experiences;
  6. To comply with applicable regulations.

We collect you personal data in various ways, as follows:

  1. When you make a reservation;
  2. When you provide feedback, ask questions, make transactions, or use our services and facilities. This includes booking a room or dining and contacting our call center;
  3. When you register for alerts or subscribe to our mailing lists or notification services;
  4. When you visit our locations, such as restaurants and beach clubs;
  5. When you register for tickets, credit facilities, or memberships relevant to our available programs;
  6. When your image or voice is recorded by cameras or other devices installed at our locations;
  7. When you use our self-service kiosks, websites, mobile applications, or interact on our social media pages;
  8. When you participate in our surveys, interviews, contests, or events;
  9. From third-party sources who may lawfully disclose your personal data to us (such as background check providers, event organizers, partners, travel and ticketing agents, or booking platforms);
  10. If required by law to collect our personal data.

The personal data we collect is, as follows:

  1. Identity: Full name, ID number, date of birth, nationality, telephone number and email address
  2. Reservations: Booking details and special preferences
  3. Payment:Credit/debit card information or other payment method
  4. Usage:Mobile application data, IP address, device and browser information
